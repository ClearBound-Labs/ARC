# ARC Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The ARC community is committed to creating an open, welcoming, and collaborative environment for researchers, developers, students, maintainers, and contributors from all backgrounds.

As contributors and community members of **ARC (Autonomous Recovery Controller)**, we pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Our goal is to build a community where ideas are evaluated on their technical merit, contributors are treated with respect, and collaboration drives innovation.

---

## Our Values

ARC is built around the following principles:

- Reliability over unnecessary complexity
- Honest and transparent technical discussion
- Evidence-driven decision making
- Respectful collaboration
- Continuous learning and knowledge sharing
- Constructive feedback and mentorship
- Open scientific and engineering inquiry

We expect all community members to uphold these values when interacting within the project.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and professional in discussions
- Providing constructive feedback during code reviews
- Helping newcomers learn and contribute
- Respecting differing technical opinions and approaches
- Sharing knowledge openly and responsibly
- Focusing discussions on improving ARC and its ecosystem
- Accepting feedback gracefully and learning from mistakes
- Giving credit to the work and contributions of others

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Trolling, baiting, or intentionally disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without explicit consent
- Deliberately misrepresenting benchmarks, results, or technical findings
- Repeatedly derailing discussions away from project goals
- Intimidation, threats, or hostile interactions
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies across all ARC community spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Community events
- Social media interactions representing ARC
- Any other project-managed communication platforms

It also applies when an individual is officially representing ARC in public spaces.

Examples include:

- Using an official ARC account
- Speaking on behalf of the project
- Acting as an appointed community representative
- Participating in official ARC events or discussions

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards outlined in this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including removing, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, or other contributions that violate these guidelines.

Enforcement decisions will be made fairly and in the best interest of the community and project.

---

## Reporting Violations

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported through:

- GitHub Issues, when appropriate
- Direct communication with project maintainers via GitHub
- Email to hello@clearboundlabs.com with the subject line "ARC"

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

The privacy and security of reporters will be respected whenever possible, and retaliation against individuals who report violations in good faith will not be tolerated.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders may use the following guidelines when determining consequences for violations.

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** Minor violation of community standards.

**Consequence:** A private explanation of the issue and guidance on expected behavior.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** Repeated minor violations or a single significant violation.

**Consequence:** A formal warning outlining the nature of the violation and expectations for future conduct.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

**Impact:** Serious misconduct or continued violations after prior warnings.

**Consequence:** Temporary suspension from participating in discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project activities.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact:** Severe misconduct, harassment, malicious behavior, or repeated violations demonstrating unwillingness to follow community standards.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from participation in ARC community spaces and project activities.
